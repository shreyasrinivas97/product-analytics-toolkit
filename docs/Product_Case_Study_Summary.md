# Product Case Study Summary
## Portfolio title
**From User Feedback to Product Priorities: Onboarding & Search Discovery**

## Challenge
A hypothetical app team needs to turn mixed user feedback into an actionable product backlog and measurable release plan.

## Approach
1. Categorized 600 synthetic feedback records by theme, sentiment, channel, and user segment.
2. Used SQL and Python to summarize feedback volume, satisfaction, and negative sentiment.
3. Created a transparent feature-prioritization score: user impact × business impact × confidence ÷ effort.
4. Drafted an MVP PRD, backlog stories, acceptance criteria, experiment plan, and QA/UAT cases.
5. Built an Excel workbook for communicating KPIs and prioritization.

## What to discuss in an interview
- Why the feature score is a decision aid rather than an objective truth.
- How you would validate the feedback themes with interviews and usability tests.
- How to define activation and search success before choosing targets.
- Why onboarding and search should ideally be tested separately.
- How you would work with Engineering and Design to reduce scope and clarify edge cases.
- How you would respond if a metric improved but a guardrail worsened.

## Evidence and limitations
The feedback and A/B records are generated synthetic data. No real users were surveyed, no live product was changed, and no actual A/B experiment was run. Competitor patterns are illustrative and require source-backed desk research before being presented as factual competitor findings.

## Suggested 5-slide portfolio walkthrough
1. Problem and user segments
2. Feedback themes and what they suggest
3. Prioritization framework and MVP scope
4. PRD + experiment metrics
5. QA plan, risks, and next steps
